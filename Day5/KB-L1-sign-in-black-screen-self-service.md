# Black Screen at Sign-In: Quick Help Steps

If your work screen stays black after sign-in or you are kicked out right away, use these steps.

Your files and saved work are safe. This problem affects the sign-in screen, not your stored documents.

## What You Can Do Now
1. Close the work access app completely.
2. Wait 2 minutes.
3. Open the app again and sign in.
4. If the screen is still black, sign out of your work account in the app, then sign in again.
5. If you can see your desktop but it stops responding, sign out of your work screen and sign in again.
6. Try one more time after waiting 5 minutes.

## If It Still Does Not Work
Contact the Service Desk if the problem continues after the steps above.

Use this line when you contact them:
Contact the Service Desk if you still see a black screen, get signed out right after sign-in, or cannot start work after two attempts.

## Details to Include in Your Help Ticket
Please share these details so support can help faster:
- Your full name
- Your work username or work email
- Best phone number to reach you
- Exact time the problem happened (include your local time, for example 9:15 AM)
- What you saw: black screen, sign-out, or frozen desktop
- Whether this happened once or more than once
- Device type (laptop or desktop)
- Location (office, home, or other)
- Internet source (office internet, home Wi-Fi, or mobile hotspot)
- A picture of the screen message (if possible)
- Whether coworkers near you have the same problem

Thank you for your patience. The support team can send your request to the right team faster when these details are included.
