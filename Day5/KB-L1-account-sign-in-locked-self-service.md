# Cannot Log In to Work: Easy Steps

If you cannot log in to work, or you see a message that your work account is locked, these steps can help.

Your files, email, and saved documents are safe. This problem can block access for now, but it does not delete your data.

## Try these steps

1. Stop trying to log in for 2 minutes.
2. Check that the `Caps Lock` key is turned off.
3. Confirm you are typing your current password exactly as usual.
4. Close the login screen or login window completely.
5. Restart your computer.
6. Make sure your internet is working.
7. Open the login screen again.
8. Enter your password slowly one time.
9. If login fails again, wait 5 minutes.
10. Try to log in one last time.

## If it still does not work

Contact the Service Desk if you still cannot log in after the steps above, if your account still shows as locked, or if you cannot do urgent work.

## Details to include in your help request

Please include the details below so support can help you faster:

- Your full name
- Your work login name or work email
- Best phone number to reach you
- Computer name (if known, for example `DESKTOP-XXXXX`)
- Your location (office, home, or other)
- The exact time the problem started (include your time zone)
- The exact message you see on screen
- Whether you completed the restart and wait steps
- Whether this happened once or multiple times
- Whether coworkers near you have the same issue
- A photo of the message (if possible)

Thank you for your patience. Sharing these details early helps the Service Desk restore access faster.
